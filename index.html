WUN n8n Private Uploads

An owner-operated connection between local n8n automation and the World Update | Newsroom YouTube channel.

What this app does

The app reads the connected YouTube channel’s identity to check that the correct WUN channel is selected. It can upload owner-approved videos to that channel through n8n. Current workflow uploads are configured as Private; scheduled and public publishing are disabled.

The automation runs on the owner’s computer. This website provides app information only. It has no sign-in form, does not collect passwords, and does not expose the local n8n instance.

Google access

The connection requests only video-upload and read-only YouTube access. Google handles account sign-in and consent. Connection credentials remain in the owner’s local n8n installation and are not included in this website.
