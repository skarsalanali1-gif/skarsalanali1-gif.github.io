Privacy policy

WUN n8n Private Uploads is an owner-operated tool for the World Update | Newsroom YouTube channel. This policy describes this integration’s use of Google data.

Access and purpose

The app requests youtube.readonly to verify the connected channel’s ID and title, and youtube.upload to upload videos approved by the owner. The app receives OAuth authorization tokens so local n8n can make those approved requests without handling the owner’s Google password.

These permissions do not give this integration access to Gmail, Google Drive, AdSense or payment information. Google sign-in and the consent screen show the permissions requested.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored by the owner’s local n8n installation using n8n’s credential encryption. Channel information, video metadata and execution records may be retained locally until the owner deletes them or the configured n8n retention process removes them. Secrets are not placed in this informational website.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used for channel verification and owner-approved uploads. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used by this integration to train generalized artificial-intelligence models. Approved video files and metadata are transmitted to YouTube when an upload is authorized. Current workflow uploads are configured as Private.

WUN n8n Private Uploads’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Owner control and removal

The owner can revoke this app’s access in their Google Account’s third-party connections settings, and remove the credential or local execution records in n8n. Revoking access stops further authorized API use; it does not itself delete videos already stored on YouTube. Those videos can be managed separately in YouTube Studio.

This information website

The site has no account registration, forms, advertising or application analytics. Its hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information to serve the pages. Do not send passwords, verification codes or OAuth tokens by email.

Contact and updates

Questions or requests about this integration can be sent to worldupdatenewsroom@gmail.com. This policy will be updated if the integration’s data practices change.
