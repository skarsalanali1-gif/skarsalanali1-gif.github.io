Terms of use

WUN n8n Private Uploads is a private, owner-operated automation connection for the World Update | Newsroom channel. It is not an open registration or paid hosted service.

Authorized use

Use is limited to the owner and people the owner explicitly authorizes. The operator must have permission to manage the connected YouTube channel and rights to upload the selected content. Use must comply with YouTube’s terms and Google’s API policies.

Approval and video visibility

The local workflow verifies the WUN channel before uploads. Current upload settings require Private visibility, and automatic schedules are inactive. A change to the OAuth app’s Google publishing status affects its authorization setup; it does not by itself change a video’s visibility or start a workflow.

Cost and availability

WUN does not charge a subscription for this private connection. The integration is operated under the owner’s $0 additional monthly cost policy. Service availability and request limits depend on Google, YouTube and the owner’s local installation. No permanent availability or never-expiring authorization is promised.

Data and access

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. The owner can stop the workflow and revoke Google access at any time. This website does not provide access to the local automation server.
